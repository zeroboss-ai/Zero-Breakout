ZeroBreakout is a high-precision algorithmic technical structure and breakout pattern detection terminal engineered specifically for active traders, swing traders, and momentum investors following the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE).

Why We Built ZeroBreakout

The Indian stock market lists over 2,200+ equities. Manually scrolling through hundreds of daily candlestick charts after market close is exhausting, prone to human bias, and takes hours.

ZeroBreakout automates this entire pipeline. Every trading day at 5:00 PM IST, our modular scanning engine ingests official NSE closing Bhavcopy archives and mathematically scans the entire quality universe across 5+ powerful institutional breakout patterns in milliseconds.
